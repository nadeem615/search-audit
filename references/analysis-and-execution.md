# Semantic review and execution

The mechanical audit collects evidence. Complete these decisions using the assistant and write analysis JSON; CLI-only runs mark semantic assessment not assessed until reviewed.

## Business and template review

Read actual pages. Identify business model, offerings, conversion goals, languages/regions and templates. Label inferred goals. Prioritise confirmed blockers and important affected pages before cosmetic changes.

Inspect each important template and anomalous group. Explain the probable cause and how to confirm it. Matching symptoms alone do not prove a shared source-code bug. Preserve intentional noindex, canonical, pagination and regional choices.

## Intent and overlap

Use content, linked GSC history and bounded current SERP evidence for the locale/device when available; record source/date. Assess whether the page format and offering answer the actual task.

Distinguish duplicate purpose from healthy multiple-result visibility, funnel stages and regional variation. Review query distribution and time-series switching. Prefer differentiation/internal links where consolidation lacks evidence. A merge requires relevance mapping, retained content, justified redirects and validation of links/sitemaps.

## AEO/GEO and external evidence

Assess accessible answers, factual support, business/entity consistency and freshness. Recommend useful original evidence and clear navigation without universal word counts or compulsory FAQ blocks.

Separate AI search and training controls. User-agent spoofing does not prove actual bot admission; use verified logs when available. Allowed crawling does not establish citations or training inclusion.

For visibility, record engine/model, prompt/query, date, locale, cited URL, response artifact and repetitions. Separate mentions, citations, referrals and outcomes. Before/after changes do not establish causation.

Use imported backlinks and bounded public evidence for relevant editorial opportunities and broken destinations. Do not invent a complete link inventory or bulk-disavow from generic risk scores.

## Analysis contract

Every content/strategic action requires action, reason and evidence references (finding IDs, audited URLs, local evidence or external sources). No credentials.

~~~json
{
  "executiveSummary": "Specific evidence-backed priorities for this business.",
  "businessContext": {
    "model": "Observed business model, with inference labelled.",
    "goals": ["Observed or user-provided goal"],
    "markets": ["Verified market/language"]
  },
  "contentActions": [{
    "url": "https://example.com/actual-page",
    "action": "Exact content or architecture change.",
    "reason": "Observed mismatch and uncertainty.",
    "priority": "p1",
    "evidence": ["finding-id", "https://example.com/actual-page"]
  }],
  "strategicActions": [{
    "action": "Specific implementation or evidence-gathering task.",
    "reason": "Decision enabled and why it matters.",
    "priority": "p2",
    "evidence": ["audit.json#coverage"]
  }],
  "evidenceLimitations": ["Missing source/coverage and effect on decisions."]
}
~~~

Replace illustrative text with completed observations. The CLI validates structure; the assistant must validate evidence and accuracy.

## Implementation packages

Provide affected URLs/templates/exceptions, measured behaviour, cause/certainty, exact change, owner/effort/dependencies, priority reasoning, acceptance checks, follow-up and rollback.

Use actual source/CMS access for authorised patches. Distinguish proposed, prepared, deployed and verified. Record preconditions and changed resources. Validate templates and exceptions before release.

Re-audit freshly using the same scope after deployment. Resolve only when acceptance criteria pass. Coverage differences, data delays, lab variation and indexing recrawls can prevent immediate conclusions. Rankings, conversions and AI citations require later observation.
